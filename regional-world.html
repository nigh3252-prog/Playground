Inspect

A depression is a potential basin, not an automatic lake. Annual precipitation/runoff, evaporation and estimated leakage determine actual standing water. Closed basins stop surface flow. This is a plausibility model, not a forecast.

Reference data

Elevation: Mapzen / AWS Terrain Tiles. USGS 3DEP, GMTED2010 and SRTM; NOAA ETOPO1. Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – Canada.

Water outlines and dashed river references: Natural Earth, public domain, v5.1.2. Cartographic references, not complete modern hydrography.

Known lakes are preserved input constraints—not independent predictions. Great Lakes have positive freshwater levels, not sea level. DEM over water represents surface, not lake-bottom depth. Cross-crop inflows and inter-lake channels are unresolved. Climate, ecology and human scores remain simulated. City labels never influence the scores.
