Data and interpretation

Previous mapped-water reference view remains available for orientation. Its prescribed lakes are not independent predictions.

Real benchmarks use raw Mapzen/AWS Terrain Tiles and interpolated NOAA station normals. Water, population and city labels are not fed to the model.

Scoring observations: USGS NHD waterbodies and NHDPlus HR streams; 2020 Census tracts; Census 1850 largest-100 urban-place sample, geolocated using modern Gazetteer internal points. That historical sample is not a full population census of these regions.

Elevation attribution: Mapzen; USGS 3DEP/GMTED2010/SRTM, NOAA ETOPO1, Canadian terrain under the Open Government Licence – Canada. Observation coverage uses public-domain Natural Earth boundaries.

Raw elevations over real lakes usually describe the water surface, not bathymetry. Failure to reconstruct such lakes is not solely a water-budget error. Climate, leakage and human-potential rules remain approximations. No settlements are generated.
