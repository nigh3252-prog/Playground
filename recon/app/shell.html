Reconstruction-by-code: every part is a generated primitive — extruded profiles for the plates and fins, capsules for the bladder pods, tori for the straps. No mesh files, no downloaded art. Cel response is a 3-step toon ramp plus an ink contour pass, matching the reference's ink-and-flat-fill medium rather than interpreting it as PBR.

The planform (wing chord, sweep and span) is inferred — a single side elevation cannot show it. Treat the top view as an informed guess, not a measurement.
