HOW TO PLAY

Snap your phone into the Backbone and open this page in Safari or Chrome. Press any button so the browser sees the controller, then tap the screen once to allow sound.

Every game uses PlayStation symbols: confirms, backs out, pauses. toggles sound, and + toggles haptics.

For true fullscreen, use Share → Add to Home Screen and launch from the icon.
