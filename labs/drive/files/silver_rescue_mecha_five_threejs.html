Silver Rescue Mecha — 5 Arm-Free Variants
Low-poly, Mega Man Legends-ish proportions; original homage shapes.
Controls: drag to orbit, pinch to zoom, two-finger drag to pan. These are intentionally shoulderless/arm-free so a separate weapon, summon arm, or pile-bunker effect can be attached later without fighting the character silhouette.
Design notes: all five borrow the silver rescue / sixth-ranger / crash-tender mecha language without copying exact helmet, suit, or zord forms. Big head, chunky boots, bright visor, and clean color blocking are tuned for top-down readability.
