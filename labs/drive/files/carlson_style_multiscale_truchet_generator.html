Carlson-Style Multi-Scale Truchet Generator

A closer remake of the layered multi-scale idea: 1/2 scaling, alternating black/white by level, edge connection points at 1/3 and 2/3, overlapping “winged” tiles, and motif rendering on top.

This remake is designed to follow the page’s logic more closely than the earlier recursive-square version: each level is half the previous scale, smaller tiles are layered above larger ones, colors invert by level, and motifs connect edge points placed at 1/3 and 2/3.
Tip: try base grid 3–5, levels 5–6, and mixed motifs for the most Carlson-ish feel.
