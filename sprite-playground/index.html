CHARACTER 04

Resonance Wander

A deliberate roaming conductor with a braced two-joint walk and crackling purple fire. It drifts, marks a resonant mote, walks to the side that suits the weapon it wants, rings the mote out of the air with the shafted focus or brings the chained one down through it, and still stops to raise both focuses into a field-wide pulse. Point anywhere on the field to mark a target for it.
