Motion notes

Concept read
  1. Little knight, heavy head. The march carries the pot's weight: the body drops onto every plant, the lid springs a beat late, and the scarf keeps swinging after the torso stops.
  2. Duty over flourish. Stride is short and repeatable with a locked forward lean — a small sentinel covering ground, not a hero posing.
  3. He is a stove. Each footfall chuffs steam from the spout, and the amber eye pulses on the beat like a pressure gauge.
  4. The spear is a pike, not a sword: it is braced across the body on the march and drives from the hip, so thrusts read as a whole-body lunge.
  5. Attacks build pressure before they spend it — coil, whip, then a hit stop and shake on the contact frame so tiny arms land with mass.
  6. Pressure Vent is the character's signature: crouch, rattle, then blow the boiler and ride the steam column upward.
