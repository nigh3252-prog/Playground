GAMMA RAMPAGE

An escalating city run. Finish 5 contracts, then reach extraction. How much chaos can you afford right now?

Controls

WASD move · Shift sprint (sprint into walls to climb)
Mouse aim · LMB punch / throw / fire
E / RMB grab · drop (cars, lampposts, crates, scientists…)
F weaponize what you hold (a bus becomes boxing gloves)
Space tap to hop · hold to charge a super-leap
Q ground pound (in the air: meteor slam)
R Gamma Surge when Rage is full
M city map · Esc pause

Controller (Backbone or any standard gamepad)

Left stick move · LT or click L3 sprint
Right stick aim (it follows your movement if you leave it alone, with light aim assist)
RT / X punch · throw · fire
A tap to hop · hold to super-leap · B ground pound / slam
Y grab · drop · LB weaponize · RB Gamma Surge
View city map · Menu pause · menus: D-pad/stick move, A select, B back, Y reroll

How it works

Destruction raises Heat. Heat brings police → SWAT → army → tanks → gunships → anti‑gamma units → experimental weapons → absurd deployments. Hide (no one sees you for 5s) and heat cools, but never below a floor set by time and your peak. Higher heat means rarer mutations, black contracts and more gamma.
The city is systemic: knock out a substation to black out a district (cameras and turrets die, and troops can't see as far), break bridges to cut off ground reinforcements, burst hydrants and drop live wires into the water, blow up gas stations, and topple buildings onto convoys. Destroying a gamma lab releases mutants who fight the army.

Banner's Lab

Permanent upgrades bought with gamma. You keep all of it on extraction and half if you fall.
